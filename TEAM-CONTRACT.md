## How we decide
Small things: Each team member will decide for themselves when it comes to minor decisions in their own part. The changes will still be communicated across the team, of course. This is in order to avoid certain misunderstandings when the entire project involves everyone.

Big things (scope, stack, pivots): Larger decisions affecting the entire project will be made by a majority vote involving all 3 of us. Avoiding potential changes that may turn out to be catastrophic.
